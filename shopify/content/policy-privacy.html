InstaMom respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how InstaMom University (“InstaMom,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you visit instamomuniversity.com, place an order, send a gift, join a mailing list, communicate with us, use our digital resources, book a service, or otherwise interact with us (collectively, the “Services”).

This Policy does not apply to third-party websites, applications, or services that have their own privacy notices. By using the Services, you acknowledge the practices described here.

1. Personal information we collect

Information you provide directly

  • Identity and contact information, such as name, email address, telephone number, billing address, shipping address, account username, and communication preferences;
  • Order and transaction information, such as items purchased, gift selections, recipient details, personalization, delivery instructions, coupon use, returns, refunds, and customer-service history;
  • Payment-related information. Payments are processed by Shopify and its payment providers. We may receive payment status, billing contact details, transaction identifiers, and limited card details such as brand and last four digits, but we do not intentionally store full payment-card numbers;
  • Account information, saved preferences, and order history, if accounts are offered;
  • Content you submit, including gift messages, reviews, survey responses, photographs, and correspondence;
  • Coaching or service information you choose to provide, such as scheduling details, goals, course-planning information, and notes needed to provide the requested service; and
  • Marketing choices and consent records, including email subscriptions and opt-out requests.

Information collected automatically

When you use the Services, our e-commerce platform collects device, browser, and usage information, including IP address, device identifiers, browser type, operating system, referring pages, pages viewed, links clicked, approximate location derived from IP address, session activity, timestamps, and cookie or similar-technology identifiers.

Information from other sources

We may receive information from our e-commerce platform and its payment providers, from shipping carriers, from fraud-prevention services, and from a purchaser who sends you an InstaMom gift.

2. Gift recipients and student information

A purchaser may provide personal information about a gift recipient or student. We use that information to fulfill the order, deliver the gift, communicate about delivery when appropriate, prevent fraud, maintain records, and provide requested services. Purchasers should provide only information they are authorized to share and should direct the recipient to this Policy when appropriate.

InstaMom is not an educational institution and does not claim that information a customer voluntarily provides is protected by the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (“FERPA”). Please do not send academic records, health records, government identification numbers, passwords, financial-account credentials, or other sensitive information unless we specifically request it through a secure method and explain why it is needed.

3. How we use personal information

  • Provide, personalize, and improve the Services;
  • Process payments, fulfill and ship orders, provide tracking, manage returns and refunds, and communicate with purchasers and recipients;
  • Create and administer accounts, bookings, coaching, digital content, customer support, and promotions;
  • Authenticate users, maintain security, prevent fraud, enforce our policies, and protect our rights and the rights of others;
  • Understand site performance, product demand, and customer experience;
  • Send transactional messages and, with any consent required by law, promotional email;
  • Comply with law, tax, accounting, recordkeeping, product-safety, and legal-process obligations; and
  • Carry out another purpose disclosed when information is collected or with your consent.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

Our e-commerce platform uses cookies and similar technologies to operate the website, remember preferences, keep a shopping cart, understand use, and prevent fraud.

We do not currently use advertising pixels, cross-site advertising tags, or third-party marketing analytics on the Services. You can control cookies through your browser or device settings. Blocking necessary cookies may prevent portions of the Services from functioning. If we add advertising or analytics technologies in future, we will update this Policy and provide any consent or opt-out mechanism required by law, including honoring a Global Privacy Control signal where applicable law requires it.

5. How we disclose personal information

We may disclose personal information to the following categories of recipients for the purposes described in this Policy:

  • Shopify, our e-commerce platform, which provides hosting, storefront, order management, and payment processing;
  • Shipping carriers, to deliver orders;
  • Product vendors or fulfillment partners when needed to complete an order;
  • Professional advisers, insurers, auditors, banks, and legal or financial service providers;
  • Government authorities, courts, law enforcement, or other parties when we believe disclosure is required or permitted by law, needed to protect safety or rights, or necessary to investigate fraud or policy violations;
  • A successor, buyer, investor, lender, or adviser in connection with a merger, financing, reorganization, asset transfer, bankruptcy, or similar business transaction; and
  • Other parties at your direction, with your consent, or as disclosed when information is collected.

6. Sale, sharing, and targeted advertising

InstaMom does not sell personal information, and does not share personal information for targeted advertising. We do not currently run advertising or social-media pixels, and we do not disclose customer information to advertising networks.

Some privacy laws define “sale,” “sharing,” and “targeted advertising” broadly. If our practices ever fall within those definitions, we will update this Policy and provide the opt-out described in Section 10.

7. Marketing communications

You may unsubscribe from promotional email by using the link in the message or contacting us. We may still send nonpromotional communications, including order, delivery, account, security, service, and policy notices. Consent to marketing is not a condition of purchase.

8. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including to provide Services, complete transactions, maintain business and tax records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, enforce agreements, and satisfy legal obligations. Retention periods vary based on the type of information, sensitivity, relationship, legal requirements, and operational need. We may retain deidentified or aggregated information where permitted by law.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information appropriate to the nature of the information and our business. No website, transmission, or storage system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting account credentials and notifying us if you suspect unauthorized access.

10. Children's privacy

The Services are intended for purchasers and users who are at least eighteen (18), or who use the Services with the involvement and permission of a parent or legal guardian. The Services are not directed to children under thirteen (13), and we do not knowingly collect personal information online directly from a child under thirteen without legally required parental consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information contrary to this section, contact instamomuniversity@gmail.com so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

11. Your privacy rights and choices

Depending on where you live and subject to legal exceptions, you may have the right to:

  • Confirm whether we process your personal information and access it;
  • Correct inaccurate personal information;
  • Delete personal information;
  • Obtain a portable copy of certain personal information;
  • Opt out of sale, sharing, targeted advertising, or certain profiling;
  • Limit certain uses or disclosures of sensitive personal information, where applicable;
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • Appeal our denial of a privacy request, where applicable; and
  • Receive equal service and pricing without unlawful discrimination for exercising a privacy right.

To submit a request, email instamomuniversity@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy Request.” Describe your request and the state where you reside. We may need to verify your identity and request details before responding. If we cannot verify a request, we may deny it as permitted by law.

An authorized agent may submit a request where permitted by law. We may require proof of authorization and may ask the consumer to verify identity or confirm the request directly. To appeal a decision, reply to our response with “Privacy Appeal” and explain the basis for the appeal.

Virginia residents whose data is covered by the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act may have rights to access, correct, delete, obtain a copy of, and opt out of certain uses of personal data, as well as a right to appeal a denied request, subject to the law's scope and exceptions.
