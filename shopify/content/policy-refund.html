Please inspect your order as soon as it arrives. If we confirm that an item was damaged, defective, incorrect, or omitted due to our error, we may, at our option and as permitted by law, replace the item, send the missing item, issue store credit, or refund the affected item and any associated standard shipping charge. Unless a product page states otherwise, an eligible physical item may be returned within fourteen (14) calendar days after delivery only if it is unopened, unused, unworn, unwashed, unaltered, in its original sealed packaging, and in resalable condition with all tags and inserts intact.

  • Original shipping, expedited shipping, gift-wrap, personalization, and handling charges are nonrefundable except when the return results from our error or the law requires a refund.
  • We recommend using a trackable return service. A return is not complete until it is received and inspected.
  • We do not accept returns sent cash-on-delivery.

A curated box is sold as a bundled product. Individual components generally may not be returned for a partial refund because of personal preference. We begin preparing orders quickly. You may request a cancellation or address change by contacting instamomuniversity@gmail.com within two (2) hours after ordering. A request is not effective until we confirm it in writing. We cannot guarantee cancellation, modification, or redirection after an order enters processing, is personalized, or is transferred to a carrier.

If we can cancel an unprocessed order, we will refund the amount paid to the original payment method. If the order has already shipped, any return is governed by this Policy. 6. Coaching and appointment-based services

Unless the booking page provides different terms, appointments may be rescheduled or canceled without charge at least twenty-four (24) hours before the scheduled start time. An appointment may be rescheduled twice; a third reschedule request voids the appointment and it is not refundable. Cancellations made less than twenty-four hours before the appointment and missed appointments may be nonrefundable. If InstaMom cancels and cannot provide a reasonable replacement time, the customer may choose rescheduling or a refund for the canceled service.

Packages of sessions expire as disclosed at purchase. Refunds are not available for sessions already delivered. Nothing in this section limits rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

After an authorized return is received and inspected, we will notify you whether the refund is approved. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method, ordinarily within five (5) to ten (10) business days after approval. Your bank or payment provider may require additional processing time. We do not control that posting period.

If a gift is returned, any refund is issued to the original purchaser and original payment method unless the law or our written agreement requires otherwise. Promotional value, discounts, and bundled pricing may be allocated proportionally when calculating a partial refund.

If tracking has not updated for an unusual period or a package is marked delivered but cannot be found, contact instamomuniversity@gmail.com promptly and no later than seven (7) calendar days after the expected or recorded delivery date. We will assist with a carrier inquiry when reasonably possible. We do not guarantee replacement or refund for theft after correctly addressed delivery, but we will evaluate the facts, carrier documentation, and applicable law.
